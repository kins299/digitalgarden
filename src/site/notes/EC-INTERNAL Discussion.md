### Platform Walkthrough

- Startup onboarding flow: KYC process, profile completion, document upload
- Thesis fit filter: predefined criteria screen startups at first level; auto-rejects non-fitting startups
- Missing data flagged automatically: incomplete profiles and documents surfaced on dashboard
- Request revision feature: sends email directly to startup with typed summary of what’s missing
- Dashboard metrics added: total committed, total deployed, investor average ticket size

### Investor Flow and Deal Room

- Prospective investor preview page: showcases startups via WhatsApp group or other channels
- Investors can view pitch deck slide-by-slide, financials, and data room documents
- System auto-generates financial details from filled profile, but actual uploaded copy required
- Soft commit flow: investor clicks commit, redirected to login, then to soft commit page
- WhatsApp message template auto-generated; can be copied and modified before posting
- Verified investor tag: admins can grant verified status to investors

### Admin Capabilities and Disbursement

- Admin portal: login with credentials, manage startup profiles and documents
- Disbursement recording: manual entry of amount, currency, date, transaction reference, and notes
- Portfolio display: deployed startups listed; clicking shows startup summary
- Co-invest referral: new investors can sign up directly via co-invest link
- Admin provision planned: admins can manually upload pitch decks, documents, and video pitch links on behalf of startups (e.g., received via WhatsApp or email)

### Partnerships and Inbound Pipeline

- Partnership executed with Startup Flora: advisory firm claiming 12,000+ startups in portfolio
  - They make startups investment-ready; not a funding guarantee
  - MOU signed; startups being enrolled into the portal
  - Ops contact from Startup Flora to liaise directly on startup flow
- Cybersecurity company partnership: technology due diligence partner
  - Has own SOC-assisted security platform (SIEM tool)
  - Will assess startups on tech and cybersec readiness
  - Relevant given DPDPA and cybersecurity compliance mandates
- Fitness certificate (not formal certification): issued to startups assessed as investment-ready on tech side
- Non-tech gaps (pitch, financials) routed to other specialist partners
- Goal: full ecosystem of partners, multiple revenue streams
