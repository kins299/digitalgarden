### BHP (Bharat Health Innovation Program)

- Focus: deep tech in healthcare (cancer care, paleo care, neonatal, emergency cell/tissue re-engineering)
- Startup pipeline sourced from Bangalore Bioinnovation Center, IIT Madras Research Park, IIT Madras
- Joint evaluation thesis with five VCs: Enlightened Capital, Unicorn India Ventures, Blue Hill VCs, Bessemer, Sonycon India Ventures
- Fund structure:
  - 20 crore each from 5 VCs = 100 crore corpus
  - 10 to 15 startups funded
  - Ticket size: 1 to 1.25 or 2M USD (approx. 9 to 15 crore per startup)
- Year-long program: evaluation and investment only, no acceleration or co-op

### One Pitch Program

- Virtual pitch room: 200 to 300 VC founders, shortlisted startups pitch to 10 to 30 VCs in one call
- Enlightened Capital co-invests minimum 25 lakhs per qualified startup
- Typical raise: 1 to 2 crore; 10 VCs at 25 to 30 lakh each = 2.5 to 3 crore per startup

### Revenue Streams

- Citrus: tech due diligence fees, technology readiness reports, tech advisory, IT compliance advisory, cloud credits (Google, AWS)
- BHP (Enlightened Capital):
  - Equity stake in invested startups
  - 20% carry on investments
  - 2% annual management fee from investors
- One Pitch (Enlightened Capital):
  - Equity + 20% carry on own investments
  - Management fee from investors
  - 5% success fee on other VCs’ investments (2.5% to Enlightened Capital)
  - Example: 1 crore investment → 2.5 lakh success fee; 5 startups/month → \~12.5 to 15 lakh/month
